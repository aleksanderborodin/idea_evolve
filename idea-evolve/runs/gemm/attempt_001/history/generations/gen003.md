## Strategic Assessment

**strategic_shift: false**

Gen003 was a **consolidation generation** with one modest score improvement and
significant negative knowledge (NT store approaches debunked, vpshufb debunked).
The most important finding is the **memory bandwidth wall** (pattern_011) — the
row-streaming kernel's compute path is near-optimal, and future improvement must
come from memory traffic reduction.

Three untested high-leverage approaches emerged for gen004:
1. SSE 128-bit NT stores (idea_021) — estimated geomean ~105 µs
2. 4-row ternlogd+popcnt kernel (idea_022) — estimated geomean ~80-95 µs
3. Multi-threading (idea_020) — estimated geomean ~105-130 µs

If combinations 1+2 or 1+2+3 succeed, the 60-80 µs range is achievable.
The 24 µs target remains physically impossible given measured bandwidth floors
(medium alone has a ~220 µs floor).
