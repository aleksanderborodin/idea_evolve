## Key Discoveries

1. **Memset elimination** (idea_010): Biggest single-step win. Removing `memset(C,0,...)` saves ~1000 µs on large benchmark. Provides 1.5-2x speedup.
2. **Vectorized pack_B** (idea_007): SIMD packing with zmm load/store was bigger than micro-kernel improvement alone.
3. **Deferred widening** (idea_004): int8/int16 accumulation instead of int32-in-hot-loop was critical for AVX-512 performance.
4. **vpternlogd** (idea_011): Fuses 6-7 bitwise ops into 2 instructions. Used by best solutions.
5. **VNNI debunked** (idea_003): Not applicable to bit-packed data format.
