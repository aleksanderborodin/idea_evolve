## Phase 2: Agent-Reported Doubts

### DOUBT-1: State of Affairs was 2 generations stale
**Status: CONFIRMED.** The SoA still said gen 1, best 148.18 µs. Gen002 consistency review failed (0 output in 31.9s). All gen003 agents wasted turns reconstructing context.
**Resolution:** New SoA written from scratch in this review.

### DOUBT-2: Exploit_1 lost 12 of 13 solution scores
**Status: CONFIRMED.** Only sol02 (141.0 µs) has a .score file. Sol03 (152 µs) and others are lost to rankings. REC-5 (auto-write .score) has been unimplemented for 2 generations.
**Resolution:** Cannot retroactively recover scores. Flagged as critical in new SoA. REC-5 must be implemented before gen004.

### DOUBT-3: 24 µs target is physically impossible
**Status: CONFIRMED by evidence.** fact_007 measured streaming write bandwidth at 24.84 GB/s. Large C = 32 MB → minimum write time = 1289 µs. Medium C = 4 MB → minimum = ~233 µs. Even with perfect optimization: geomean of (3.0, 233, 1289) = ~99 µs theoretical floor. With threading: ~60-80 µs floor. 24 µs requires breaking bandwidth physics.
**Resolution:** Flagged in SoA Open Questions. User must confirm realistic target.

### DOUBT-4: Assembly inspection never performed
**Status: CONFIRMED.** Zero assembly inspection across 3 generations. All 4 gen003 code-producing agents requested it.
**Resolution:** Flagged in SoA as biggest blind spot. Recommend experimentator assignment in gen004.

### DOUBT-5: fact_004 port assignments are wrong
**Status: CONFIRMED by fact_008.** vpopcntb is port 0/1 (0.5c tput), not port 5 (1c tput). vpbroadcastb is the actual port 5 bottleneck.
**Resolution:** Added deprecation notice to fact_004. fact_008 is authoritative.

### DOUBT-6: 141.0 µs improvement may be compiler noise
**Status: PLAUSIBLE but unresolvable.** pattern_009 (compiler layout sensitivity) and explore_1's reported 30-40% variance support this. Without assembly comparison between 147.26 and 141.0 solutions, we cannot determine if the improvement is real.
**Resolution:** Flagged in SoA. The 141.0 score is accepted as best-measured but noted as potentially fragile.
