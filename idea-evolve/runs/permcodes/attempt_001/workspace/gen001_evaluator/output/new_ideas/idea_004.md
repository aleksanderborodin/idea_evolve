Beyond AGL(1,8), other subgroups of S₈ may yield larger or different codes:
- AΓL(1,8): semidirect product with Frobenius automorphism (GF(8) field automorphism, squaring map)
- PGL(2,7): projective general linear group on GF(7)
- PSL(2,7): simple group of order 168

The Frobenius automorphism in GF(8) is the map x → x². The coset construction approach could find novel orbit combinations not accessible via AGL(1,8).
