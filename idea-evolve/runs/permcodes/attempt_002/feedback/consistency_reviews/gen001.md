## Phase 2: Agent-Reported Doubts (Priority Investigation)

### Doubt 1: "PGL(2,7) was never implemented despite being identified in gen 1 research"
**Source:** Multiple agent reports (research_1, evaluator, system_critic)
**Investigation:** Checked all solution files — no PGL implementation found.
**Resolution:** CONFIRMED. This is the single biggest research-to-execution gap. research_1 produced a complete implementation sketch; no agent executed it. Marked as open question in SoA.

### Doubt 2: "The compatible-permutation count for the 616-code was never run"
**Source:** evaluator.md (line 47), research_1 debrief
**Investigation:** No solution in the population computes this.
**Resolution:** CONFIRMED. This is the critical empirical question — does any permutation outside the 11 AGL orbits extend the 616-code? Never answered. Marked as open question in SoA.

### Doubt 3: "evaluator knowledge not persisted"
**Source:** system_critic_debrief.md (line 94)
**Investigation:** Checked `knowledge/ideas/` — ideas DO exist there. Checked `knowledge/clusters/` — only README exists. Checked `knowledge/patterns/` — pattern files exist.
**Resolution:** PARTIALLY CONFIRMED. Ideas and patterns were persisted. Clusters were NOT persisted (0 files despite evaluator claiming 3 clusters created). The system_critic's finding about evaluator knowledge being "stranded" was partially correct for clusters.

### Doubt 4: "LP upper bound of 926 may not be tight"
**Source:** evaluator.md (line 64)
**Investigation:** The 926 figure comes from Smith & Montemanni's LP relaxation. No agent has verified whether this bound is actually tight.
**Resolution:** UNRESOLVED. The LP bound is widely cited but unverified in our pipeline. If we find a construction yielding >616, we should verify whether it approaches 926.

### Doubt 5: "Two solutions crashed due to trivial bugs"
**Source:** evaluator.md (line 157)
**Investigation:** gen001_explore_2_sol03 (GA, dtype error) and gen001_explore_2_sol04 (ILNS, timeout bug) are both marked INVALID.
**Resolution:** CONFIRMED AND FLAGGED. Both bugs are trivial (np.array([]) float64 default; greedy logic error returning all 40320 perms). GA crossover operator is marked "disputed" with confidence 0.2. ILNS bug was in sol04 only; sol05 recovered.

### Doubt 6: "AGL orbit clique was attempted 6 times — homogeneity risk"
**Source:** system_critic_debrief.md (line 100)
**Investigation:** Coverage matrix confirms idea_001 tried 6 times, all at 616.
**Resolution:** CONFIRMED. This was predictable per the architect's own risk note. The coverage matrix shows this clearly. For gen 2, the architect should actively prevent redundant AGL attempts.
