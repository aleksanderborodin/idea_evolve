## Strategic Shift

**YES — strategic_shift: true**

The gen_002 compression breakthrough (44114 vs 46312) demonstrates that the
Megaminx Cayley graph has algebraic structure exploitable for compression.
This was not anticipated — gen_001's state_of_affairs predicted compression ceiling
at ~44100 and search as the only path. The actual finding: compression CAN break
through that ceiling with algebraic identities, and the path to 15000 now requires:

1. **Compression first:** empirical identities → ~44114
2. **Beam search second:** trained predictor-guided beam to find paths compression misses
3. **Both needed:** Neither alone reaches 15000

The coverage matrix reveals that idea_008 (trained MLP predictor) has been tried ZERO
times. This is the critical experiment for gen 3.
