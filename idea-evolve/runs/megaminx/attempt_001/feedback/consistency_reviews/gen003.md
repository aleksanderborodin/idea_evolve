1. **No facts directory.** Key structural facts are spread across patterns. A dedicated facts/ directory with verified numerical claims (BFS layer sizes, growth rate, compression ratio, etc.) would be more durable. Low priority.

2. **score_progression.md missing gen003.** The file shows gen000-gen002 but not gen003=44094. This is the orchestrator's responsibility during finalize, not the consistency reviewer's.

3. **cayleypy API claims unverified independently.** The most impactful findings (MITM integration, BFS layer sizes, embedding architecture) come from a single research agent. No other agent has independently verified them. If research_1 made errors, they propagate through ideas 010-013 and the combined recipe recommendation. 4. **Gap to target analysis is sobering.** After 3 generations and ~10 hours of compute: 50572 → 44094 = 478 points improved out of 29094 needed. At this rate, ~180 more generations required. The current approach (compression + predictor beam) may be fundamentally insufficient for reaching 15000. The system should explore whether Kaggle top solutions use a qualitatively different paradigm.

5. **GNN predictors mentioned but never investigated.** research_1 mentioned them, explore_2 wished for them. A GNN that operates on the permutation structure directly could capture more information than a flat MLP (even with embeddings). Entirely unexplored.
