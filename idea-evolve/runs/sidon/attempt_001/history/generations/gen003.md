## Key Findings

### 1. Singer q=101 perturbation is definitively closed
exploit_1 tested large-k perturbation (k=5-25) with strategic and random removal across
4000+ trials. No improvement over 102. Combined with gen 2's k=1-5 exhaustive search,
perturbation is proven futile across the entire k spectrum. Experimentator_1 proved the
structural reason: minimum 45 blockers per non-member element.

### 2. Non-algebraic search ceiling established at 69
Fibonacci ordering greedy achieves 69 (new record). This is the best non-algebraic result
across 3 generations. The hierarchy: random(63) < greedy(66) < LNS(67) < SA(68) < Fibonacci(69) < ET(75) < Singer(102).

### 3. SA is debunked across all seed types
SA now fails from Singer q=97 (99), Singer q=101 (102), and Fibonacci greedy (68).
Violation-relaxed SA also fails. The swap neighborhood is structurally disconnected for
Sidon sets at competitive sizes.

### 4. Singer saturation is universal
Truncated Singer sets for ALL primes tested (q=97-113) have zero addable elements.
This deep rigidity property means no Singer-based extension approach can work.

### 5. Research agent failed again
research_1 was terminated before completing web searches for F(10000). This is the third
consecutive generation without published best-known results. Critical knowledge gap persists.

### 6. Min-blocking greedy is fundamentally broken
explore_1/sol02 implemented a "min-blocking" candidate selection that tracks conflicts via a
heuristic array but does NOT actually verify the Sidon property (unique pairwise differences)
when adding elements. Result: 775 elements with 280,849 violations. The algorithm would need
to incorporate a proper Sidon check (used_diffs set) to be valid. Took 1728s (29 min) to evaluate.
