- **Severity**: Medium — the finding is real but the evidence list was inflated.

#### idea_003/011 — TTA Debunked (Correct)
- Both correctly debunked. idea_011 confidence (0.95) is higher than idea_003 (0.0). This is acceptable — they cover the same finding at different confidence levels.
