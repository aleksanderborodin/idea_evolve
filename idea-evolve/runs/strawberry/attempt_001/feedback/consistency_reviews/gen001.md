## Phase 2: Agent-Reported Doubts (Priority Investigation)

### Doubt 1: "copy_paste=0.5 is the confirmed winner" — CONTRADICTED
- **Source**: research_1.md
- **Finding**: description.md says 0.5 is the confirmed winner, but gen1 clearly shows 0.7 outperforms 0.5 (0.8296 vs 0.8103).
- **Resolution**: The description.md refers to from-scratch training (Mode 2). For fine-tuning from converged WEIGHTS_EXP5, 0.7 is better. Not a knowledge base error — it's a scope documentation issue. The State of Affairs now clarifies this distinction.

### Doubt 2: WEIGHTS_EXP5 per-class breakdown unknown — UNRESOLVED
- **Source**: evaluator.md, research_1.md, system_critic_debrief.md
- **Finding**: No agent knows the per-class mAP50 for WEIGHTS_EXP5. Angular Leafspot weakness (0.66-0.74) is established but baseline mAP50 for each class is unknown.
- **Resolution**: UNRESOLVED. Critical missing baseline. This is now the top Open Question.

### Doubt 3: yolo11s potential unknown — UNRESOLVED
- **Source**: explore_2.md, system_analysis/gen001.md
- **Finding**: Training was healthy (trending 0.81 at epoch 40) but eval crashed with BrokenPipeError during GPU lock cleanup.
- **Resolution**: UNRESOLVED. The BrokenPipeError is an evaluate.py bug, not a model capacity issue. yolo11s ceiling is unknown.

### Doubt 4: Proxy metric reliability — UNRESOLVED
- **Source**: architect.md, system_analysis/gen001.md, pattern_003
- **Finding**: 20-epoch proxy may not predict 100-epoch ordering. The gap (0.82 proxy vs 0.92 target) could mean proxy is pessimistic, or could mean approaches that win at 20 epochs don't maintain advantage.
- **Resolution**: UNRESOLVED. The extended 40-epoch run (full_1_sol02 at 0.8209) suggests some stability in ordering, but not conclusive.

### Doubt 5: Research findings not persisted — VERIFIED BUG
- **Source**: evaluator_debrief.md, system_analysis/gen001.md
- **Finding**: research_1 output/report.md was never written to knowledge/research/gen001/. The directory exists but is empty.
- **Resolution**: Verified bug. The orchestrator's move_research_outputs() may not have been called, or research_1 never wrote to the correct path. The System Critic flagged this and it's recorded in Open Questions.

### Doubt 6: "copy_paste=0.7 advantage may not hold at 40+ epochs" — UNRESOLVED
- **Source**: state_of_affairs.md
- **Finding**: Only tested at 20 epochs.
- **Resolution**: UNRESOLVED. The description.md notes copy_paste lags at epoch 20-40 for from-scratch training, but this may not apply to fine-tuning from converged weights.

### Doubt 7: YOLO11 loss injection API unknown — UNRESOLVED
