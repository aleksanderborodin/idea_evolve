## Directive
Research the mathematical background of the first autocorrelation inequality. Produce a findings report covering:

1. **Problem origin and known results.** Where does this problem come from? What are the best known upper and lower bounds on C? Who proved them and what techniques did they use? The known range is 1.28 <= C <= 1.5098.

2. **Optimal function properties.** What is known about the shape of the extremal function (the function that achieves the minimum C)? Is it symmetric? Smooth? Compactly supported within [-1/4, 1/4] or does it use the full domain? Are there conjectured closed-form solutions?

3. **Connection to Sidon sets and additive combinatorics.** The problem is related to Sidon sets. How does this connection inform the structure of good solutions? Can discrete Sidon set constructions be lifted to continuous functions?

4. **Optimization landscape.** Is the objective C(f) known to be convex, quasi-convex, or have multiple local minima? Are there known symmetry-breaking phenomena?

5. **Practical recommendations.** Based on the math, what function families and optimization strategies are most promising for achieving C <= 1.5053?

Write your findings to your report. Do not write solution code -- your job is research only.
