## Key Findings

1. **Triplet perturbation is the new frontier technique.** explore_1 achieved the new overall best
   via gradient-guided triplet moves (d1+d2+d3=0). This is the first multi-element improvement
   over a coordinate-descent-optimized solution. New idea_021 created.

2. **Single-element coordinate descent has essentially converged.** Three independent agents
   (exploit_1, exploit_2, full_1) all show sharp convergence with sub-1e-9 improvements.
   Pattern_012 documents the exponential decay. The gen 6 claim of "1800 improvements/round,
   NOT converging" was premature.

3. **LP refinement failed for the third time — fundamental obstacle identified.** full_1 solved
   the engineering bottleneck (vectorized construction <0.01s) but exposed the flat
   autoconvolution plateau (~6500 near-max points). Few-constraint LP cannot control the
   plateau. Downsampled LP directions don't transfer. idea_020 demoted to DISPUTED.

4. **AlphaEvolve arrays tested.** exploit_2 ran coord descent on 3 AlphaEvolve arrays:
   - N=600 (C=1.5040): ZERO improvements — true coordinate-wise local minimum.
   - N=984 (C=1.5036): 2770 improvements to C=1.503550 (still improving).
   - N=1444 (C=1.5035): 2912 improvements to C=1.503476 (still improving).
   AlphaEvolve arrays at N≥984 are improvable but converge to ~1.5035, not competitive with
   TTT-Discover 30k.

5. **Experimentator delivered 3 new helpers:** incremental_autoconv_update, cross_convolution_f64,
   lp_matrix. README updated. These will save future agents significant reimplementation time.
