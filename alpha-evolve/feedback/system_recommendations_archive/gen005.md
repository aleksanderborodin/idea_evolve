## Priority 6 — Add LP-based refinement as a research target

**What to change:** Assign a research_1 agent in Gen 6 to:

> "**Implement LP-based constraint relaxation on the TTT-Discover array.**
> The autocorrelation inequality C(f) = max(f★f) / integral(f)² <= 1.5029 holds at
> near-equality for many constraints. An LP identifies which constraints are near-tight
> and finds descent directions. Even a simplified version (identify 10 tightest constraints,
> solve for joint improvement direction) may find improvements gradient methods cannot.
> This is the only method that has improved solutions at this quality level (see idea_016)."

Also: request the Evaluator or a research agent to create `helpers/autoconv_analysis.py`
that returns autoconvolution peak position, contributing element pairs, and constraint
tightness — required for any LP-based approach.

**Why:** Five agent reports across two generations named LP as the only viable path forward.
It has never been attempted by any agent in this pipeline. The engineering barrier is real
but the potential is high — LP is the method that produced both AlphaEvolve (1.5032) and
TTT-Discover (1.5029) improvements.

**Expected impact:** If successful, likely improvement of ~0.001 or more. If it fails,
closes the LP hypothesis and forces exploration of fundamentally different approaches.
