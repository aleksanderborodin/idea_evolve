## Phase 2: Agent-Reported Doubts

### Doubt 1: Pattern_021 understates drift (Evaluator)
**Investigation**: pattern_021 says "~1.4e-12/round" and recommends "resync every 1-5 rounds." Gen 11 exploit_2 showed 2000+ mods/round causes intra-round drift exceeding improvement scale. pattern_027 corrects this.
**Resolution**: CONFIRMED. pattern_021 is incomplete. pattern_027 supersedes drift guidance. Recommend annotating pattern_021 to point to pattern_027.

### Doubt 2: idea_007 and idea_016 approaching staleness (Evaluator)
**Investigation**: idea_007 last actively used gen 6 (5 gens ago = staleness threshold). idea_016 never implemented in pipeline, evidence is literature-only.
**Resolution**: CONFIRMED as approaching stale. Both remain factually correct. idea_007 is essential for GD paradigm but irrelevant to current frontier. idea_016 provides context but is not actionable. Neither should be downgraded — they are historical pillars, not active directions.

### Doubt 3: exploit_1 debrief was pre-completion template (System Critic)
**Investigation**: System critic noted TBD placeholders throughout exploit_1's debrief. Solution-idea map shows "no scored solution." Agent_gaps confirms no .score file.
**Resolution**: CONFIRMED. exploit_1 knowledge is limited to: per-round FFT resync works but single-pass CD is 100x slower; entrypoint non-reproducibility independently confirmed. No quantitative results recoverable.

### Doubt 4: topk_screened_cd not tested at N=30000 (System Critic)
**Investigation**: Experimentator_1 report says 14/14 tests at N=1000. No N=30000 test evidence.
**Resolution**: NOTED. Flag in SoA as untested at production scale. Gen 12 should validate before relying on it.

### Doubt 5: Non-reproducible entrypoints (pattern_028)
**Investigation**: exploit_1 got C=1.5028628681754832, exploit_2 got C=1.5028628681772360, both from gen010/explore_2/sol01.py which cached at 1.5028628681165177. Gap: ~6e-11.
**Resolution**: CONFIRMED by two independent agents. Must bake arrays as numpy literals. Critical for gen 12.

### Doubt 6: Accumulated drift may invalidate prior gen improvements (gen 10 System Critic)
**Investigation**: Gen 10 critic noted "drift is 3.5x the real improvement" and "prior generations' CD results may have been partially phantom." Gen 11 pattern_027 confirms intra-round drift problem. However, all scored solutions pass validate.py which uses full FFT — the final scores are trustworthy even if intermediate tracking was drifted.
**Resolution**: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED. Intermediate improvement counts may be inflated by drift, but final validated scores are accurate. The trajectory narrative is not affected — only per-round improvement claims in agent reports may be overstated.
