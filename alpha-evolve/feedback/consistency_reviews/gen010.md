## Phase 2: Agent-Reported Doubts

### Doubt 1: "Is the current technique portfolio approaching its limit?"
**Source:** Gen 9 State of Affairs, Open Question 1.
**Investigation:** Gen 10 exploit_1 found 371k improvements over 71 rounds with no decay. 1e-14 improvements increasing. Float64 precision floor NOT reached.
**Resolution:** PARTIALLY RESOLVED. CD is not exhausted at current precision. BUT practical C improvement per round is ~3-5e-13, limited by drift. Improvement rate is real but diminishing in absolute value.

### Doubt 2: "Minimax perturbation viability" (idea_023)
**Source:** Gen 9 State of Affairs, Open Question 2.
**Investigation:** Gen 10 explore_1 implemented full minimax LP with K=28 plateau positions. 68,450 trials, 0 improvements. All t* >= 0 (LP infeasible for improvement).
**Resolution:** RESOLVED — DEBUNKED. Solution is locally minimax-optimal for integral-preserving moves. The convex hull of gradient vectors at 28 plateau positions contains the origin, so no direction reduces all simultaneously.

### Doubt 3: "Interleaving order" (pattern_020)
**Source:** Gen 9 State of Affairs, Open Question 3.
**Investigation:** Gen 10 exploit_2 ran controlled A/B test. Path A (CD only): 19 improvements, C=1.5028628682225948. Path B (triplets+quads+CD): 0+0+17 improvements, C=1.5028628682226506. Path A wins by 5.57e-14.
**Resolution:** RESOLVED. Ultra-fine CD alone is strictly superior. No interleaving needed. Protocol updated in new State of Affairs.

### Doubt 4: "fact_002 outdated"
**Source:** Gen 9 State of Affairs, Open Question 4; flagged by 6+ generations of critics.
**Investigation:** fact_002 states upper bound C <= 1.5098, target C <= 1.5053. Pipeline best is C = 1.5028628681165177, beating target by 0.0024.
**Resolution:** CONFIRMED OUTDATED. Updated fact_002 not in scope (facts are read-only), but flagged for operator action. The State of Affairs now reflects actual achievement.

### Doubt 5: "score_progression.md stopped updating at gen 7"
**Source:** Gen 9-10 critics.
**Investigation:** Cannot verify orchestrator code from this review. The file shows gens 5-7 as identical "1.502863" due to 4-decimal precision, masking real progress.
**Resolution:** UNRESOLVED. Operator/orchestrator issue. Flagged in Open Questions.

### Doubt 6: "Incremental drift reliability"
**Source:** Gen 10 exploit_1 discovery.
**Investigation:** Drift quantified at ~1.4e-12/round (~2.7e-16 per accepted modification). After 25k modifications, accumulated error 3.5x larger than real improvement.
**Resolution:** RESOLVED for gen 10 (FFT resync implemented). Prior-gen improvements (gens 5-9, no resync) may include phantom improvements. Magnitude is small enough (~1e-12) that rankings and relative ordering are unaffected.
