## 2. No Assembly-Level Analysis

**Gap**: A targeted experiment to compile the best solution, dump assembly (`-S` flag), and analyze the micro-kernel hot loop would reveal concrete optimization opportunities.

## 3. NC=512 Regression Unexplained

**Gap**: Need `perf stat` analysis comparing NC=256 vs NC=512 runs to identify the specific microarchitectural bottleneck (L1 misses? dTLB misses? port contention?).
